# AI full-game audit — every move of six AI seats checked against the LRR

Twelve full 6-player all-AI games were played under `scripts/ai-monitor.ts` (`npm run ai:monitor -- 12`).
For every one of the ~16,000 moves the harness:

- re-derived movement reachability **independently** of the engine's own path search (LRR 58 move value,
  LRR 10/57/81 anomalies, LRR 1659.2 no moving through other players' ships, gravity-rift step refund);
- tracked the R3.2 secondary protocol seat by seat (answers in turn order, each other seat exactly once,
  the holder never answering their own card, the window closing back onto the holder);
- tracked the R10 agenda protocol (one vote per seat, Nekro never votes, laws actually applied to
  `activeAgendas` / `lawOwners` / planet attachments);
- checked physical invariants after every move: duplicate technologies, VP monotonicity, unit-id
  uniqueness, per-seat unit-stock conservation (nothing created from nothing, nothing vanishing —
  LRR 17.6), fleet pool and capacity per system (LRR 27.2/91), hand limit (LRR 112), non-negative pools,
  double activation (LRR 57.3), custodians token (LRR 24.1), action-card bookkeeping (LRR 66);
- collected the engine's own log lines that admit a rule is only half-wired;
- detected deadlocks, chooser crashes and no-progress loops.

The live per-run output is `docs/ai-monitor-report.md` (regenerated by the script). This file is the
audit's problem list: what was found, what it violated, and what was done about it.

## Problems found — all fixed

### 1. Arms Reduction silently destroyed ships permanently (units vanished from the game)
- **Found**: seed 1, round 4 — the `For` outcome destroyed dreadnoughts that were never returned to the
  reinforcements; a seat's dreadnought stock dropped 5 → 3 and never recovered. A stale `players`
  snapshot in the resolver (`return { ...next, players }`) discarded everything `destroyUnits` had
  recorded, and the resolver also removed ships with a raw filter instead of `destroyUnits`.
- **Rule**: LRR 17.6 (destroyed units are returned to the reinforcements); LRR agenda card text.
- **Fallout**: the seat's fleet could then sit over capacity for the rest of the game, which later made
  the Warfare primary unplayable and stalled the game.
- **Fix**: resolver uses `destroyUnits` + `trimCargo` per system and returns `next` itself. Regression
  test in `agendas.test.ts`.

### 2. A PDS-only space cannon volley left the defender's fleet permanently over capacity
- **Found**: seed 12, round 10 — a deep-space-cannon volley (PDS II in adjacent systems, LRR 2175.3)
  destroyed a home fleet's only capacity ship; the uncarried fighters stayed on the board forever because
  `afterSpaceCannonOnly` trimmed only the active player's cargo.
- **Rule**: LRR 91 (capacity) / R4.1 step 4 (excess cargo destroyed when the combat ends).
- **Fix**: `afterSpaceCannonOnly` trims the defender's cargo as well. Regression test in
  `spaceCannon.test.ts` (fails without the fix: 3 fighters survive; passes with it: 0).

### 3. Warfare primary could be offered and then rejected by its own handler
- **Found**: seed 1, round 6 — `legalMoves` offered a Warfare primary whose token redistribution left the
  seat's fleet over its fleet pool; `strategic()` rejected it ("R4.4: redistribution would exceed the
  fleet pool"), and with no recovery the game stalled.
- **Rule**: game-rules.md 4.4/R6 — "a redistribution that leaves a fleet over its pool is rejected"
  (the repo's binding test); LRR 27.2. The engine instead auto-destroyed the cheapest excess ships —
  destroying units the player never chose to lose.
- **Fix**: `withFleetPoolIntact` now rejects a distribution that strands a fleet over its pool or
  capacity; it never destroys ships on the player's behalf.

### 4. The Warfare secondary offered dock-produced infantry to the Arborec
- **Found**: seed 8, round 3 — the enumerator offered `units: { infantry: 2 }` to the Arborec, whose
  space docks cannot produce infantry (Mitosis); the handler refused it and the game stopped.
- **Rule**: Mitosis (lrr-factions.md, "Arborec space docks cannot produce infantry"); the ti4-rules
  skill's rule for "cannot" abilities: remove the option from the enumeration, never offer moves that fail.
- **Fix**: the Warfare secondary enumeration skips infantry for the Arborec, and the AI's produce filler
  no longer builds an infantry order for them in its empty-order fallback.

### 5. A blockaded Floating Factory vanished from the game
- **Found**: a Saar Floating Factory destroyed by an arriving enemy fleet (lrr-factions.md 2105.11) was
  filtered out of the system without returning it to the reinforcements — the seat's FF stock dropped
  3 → 2 permanently, so it could never be rebuilt through Construction.
- **Rule**: LRR 17.6 (destroyed units return to the reinforcements).
- **Fix**: `moveShips` returns the abandoned Floating Factory to the reinforcements.

### 6. The Technology secondary offered the same technology twice in the UI
- **Found** in the live BADJAT investigation this audit grew out of: the same tech id appeared in the
  secondary panel's option list once plain and once via a tech-skip planet, and the duplicated option was
  part of what let a duplicate acquisition through in play.
- **Rule**: LRR 2611.1 (a player owns each technology once).
- **Fix**: `SecondaryPanel.tsx` dedupes the tech options before rendering.

## Remaining open problems

### A. Laws the engine attaches but does not enforce

~~Holy Planet of Ixth~~ and ~~Demilitarized Zone~~ — both are now enforced (see below); the
`law-not-enforced` monitor category is empty as of the 24-seed rerun.

- **Holy Planet of Ixth** — enforced: the planet's own space dock produces nothing (`productionLimit`
  skips it), and every control change of the planet swings 1 VP (`holyPlanetControlSwing`, clamped at
  zero per LRR 25). Discarding the law takes no VP back (lrr-components.md, Holy Planet 1).
- **Demilitarized Zone** — enforced: units cannot land there (`land` + `landablePlanets`), cannot be
  produced onto it (`produce`, `productionLimit`), and nothing may be placed there (Construction,
  Mitosis, Colonial Redistribution all refuse it).

### B. The main branch is committed red
Pre-existing on `main` (commit `7b0a667`), independent of the audit:

- **147 engine tests fail** — e.g. the R4.4 Warfare-primary redistribution test and the whole
  `movement.test.ts` suite reference map systems (`bereg`, `quann`) that the generated test galaxy does
  not contain, plus other fallout from the last large commit. The audit's fixes add no new failures
  (verified by running the suite with and without the changes) and add two regression tests that fail on
  the unfixed code and pass with it.
- **39 TypeScript errors in test files** (`npx tsc -p tsconfig.app.json --noEmit`), mostly missing
  imports and legacy `tradePost` fixtures. Non-test source compiles clean.

## Result after the fixes

All 12 games (seeds 1–12, three 6-faction sets) run to completion within the move budget. The only
monitor findings left are the two half-wired laws above, which the engine states in its own log.

## Follow-up session — 2026-09-15 (afternoon)

The monitor kept running as the regression gate for new work; every fix below was verified against it
(12–24 full games, zero findings after each).

### Fixed

- **Objective deck did not follow the LRR** — one objective revealed at setup instead of two (LRR 1785),
  and the reveal at the end of the status phase instead of the start, so the deck dried two rounds late
  and games ran to round 10: a player won with 9 VP through the "most victory points" end (LRR 2896.8)
  two scoring rounds after the LRR says the game ends. Now: two Stage I revealed at setup, the reveal at
  the status phase's start (scoreable the same phase), and a dry deck ends the game immediately before
  scoring. Games last 8 scoring rounds and end during round 9's status phase.
- **Losing a space dock stranded the orphaned fighters** — an invasion that took a home planet returned
  the dock to the loser's reinforcements without trimming the fighters its free slots were covering; the
  board then sat failing checkFleet for the rest of the game (339 flagged moves in one batch). Any dock
  destruction now trims the fleets in its system (LRR 91, R4.1 step 4).
- **Fleet Regulations For left over-pool fleets standing** — every fleet must fit the new pool before any
  other effect resolves (lrr-components.md 2317); the cheapest excess ships are now destroyed and trimmed.
- **Shard/Crown transfers drove VP negative** (LRR 25) — clamped at zero.
- **The activation step closed doors a held card opens** — tiles in Flank Speed / Lost Star Chart /
  In The Silence Of Space range were dimmed "No ship in range" because the warning ignored the seat's
  hand; those cards are played into the activation's own reaction window, so the far system is exactly
  the one the player must be able to choose. The board now says "In range with Flank Speed" instead.

### Wired (were display-only)

- **Xxcha Peace Accords** — after resolving Diplomacy, take control of an empty planet adjacent to one
  you control (gained exhausted; Mecatol excluded while the custodians token stands; the FAQ's
  explore-on-gain is not implemented and the log says so).
- **Xxcha Transit Diodes** — component action relocating up to 4 ground forces from systems holding the
  seat's command token (planet or space area) to planets they control; readies in the status phase.

### UX

- Seat manifest rebalanced (the commander column no longer hogs the row; faction dropdown fits its
  longest name; fleet/techs get the room) and hovering a seat's faction shows the faction's full card
  (17 faction sheets vendored from the TI4 wiki).
- The Ixthian Artifact's Speaker die roll is now its own ceremonial block in the agenda result (large
  value, glowing on a 6+, "the Artifact is silent" on a miss) instead of a line buried in the log box.
